Sales Guide

Quick help for owner

01 • Select Product
Choose the steel product that was sold from the product dropdown.
02 • Enter Quantity
Enter the number of units sold. Do not enter a quantity greater than available stock.
03 • Check Total
The estimated sale amount is calculated automatically from product price × quantity.
04 • Record Sale
Click Record Sale. The transaction is saved and inventory stock is updated.
