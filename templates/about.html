NATIONAL STEEL

About the Management System

National Steel Owner Management System is a private business management platform designed to manage products, inventory, sales and business reports from one central dashboard.

About National Steel

A simple and professional system for managing day-to-day steel business operations.

National Steel is supported by a dedicated owner management system that keeps important business information organized in one place.

The system allows the owner to manage products, monitor available stock, record sales and review sales history without maintaining separate manual records.

Business reports provide a quick overview of sales performance, inventory health, product categories and low-stock items.
